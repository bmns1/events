Good to Know

🧺 Come comfortable
Bring a picnic blanket or folding chairs — it's an open park, so seating is bring-your-own.
❄️ Keep it fresh
A cooler bag or insulated carrier keeps cold dishes cold and hot dishes hot until we eat.
🥄 Serving spoon
Please bring a serving utensil with your dish — it makes the buffet line so much easier.
🏷️ Label it
A small card with your dish name and ingredients helps families with allergies choose safely.
☀️ Sun & shade
August in Sunnyvale is warm — sunscreen, hats, and plenty of water are a good idea.
🧹 Leave it lovely
Let's leave the park better than we found it. Trash bags and helping hands are always welcome.
